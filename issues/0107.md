## Description

Optional: honour the `priority` header and PRIORITY_UPDATE frames (type 0xF0700) on the control stream to order response data. The HTTP/2 half stays in #0065.

## Scope

- Shared `priority` structured-field parser (urgency 0–7, incremental) in `HTTPCore`, reused by #0065.
- PRIORITY_UPDATE for request streams validated on the control stream (`H3_ID_ERROR` for bad IDs).
- Scheduling by urgency then round-robin among incremental streams, implemented as a send-order hint since QUIC scheduling is owned by Network.framework; document the limits of what can be controlled.

## Acceptance criteria

- Unit tests for the parser and for scheduling order with mixed urgencies.
- The resolution states how much control NW QUIC gives (measured, not assumed).
