## Description

Confirm real clients use HTTP/3 and measure it (#0062 widened to h3).

## Scope

- Browser check with the local CA trusted (#0072): Safari, plus Chrome and Firefox if installed; load the sample site, confirm the protocol column shows `h3` after `Alt-Svc` upgrade, hard reload (304 over h3), large download while navigating.
- `URLSession` integration test with `assumesHTTP3Capable` and a trust override fetching over h3 (`URLSessionTaskMetrics` reports `h3`).
- Load: `sws-bench` h3 suite driving Homebrew curl or an NW QUIC client at concurrency 1/16/128 for small and large files; compare to HTTP/1.1 over TLS; results under `docs/benchmarks/`.

## Acceptance criteria

- The `URLSession` h3 test runs in `swift test` (skipped with a message when the identity is missing).
- Browser findings are recorded in the resolution; any bugs are filed separately.
- Benchmark JSON committed; no regression over 10% on HTTP/1.1 numbers versus the previous milestone.
